# Working text carries the binding consequence

Crossing complete image and working-text states after the first joint block makes the final color assignment follow text in all eight new hybrid continuations. Successful-side text transfers the assignment under the other image state; base-side text removes it under the successful image state. The paraphrase retains its partial purple-sphere/red-cube response. This is conditional carrier evidence within four historical, source-assisted contexts and two already primed receiving image states.

The [route/payload factorial](FLUX-BINDING-ROUTE-PAYLOAD.md) established that the same delivered direction behaves differently at different receiving rows. This experiment follows that consequence forward. It refines the existing contextual-object-binding family rather than discovering a new mathematical primitive.

## Paired source states and frozen prediction

The specimen is the original FLUX.2 Klein4B distilled checkpoint, CUDA BF16, batch 1, 512×512, four denoising steps and guidance 1. Clocks are zero based. The contexts are the red-sphere/blue-cube scene at seeds 109 and 347, the reversed-mention paraphrase at seed 109, and red cone/blue cylinder at seed 109. These are three distinct paired conditioning inputs and two seeds, with dependent historical contrasts.

P is the actual post-joint0 state of prior A@A: the successful attribute direction delivered to its original attribute rows. Q is the actual post-joint0 state of prior A@B: the same direction moved to later contextual rows at matched delivered text norm. P previously produced three clear assignment transfers and a partial paraphrase; Q retained the base assignment. Both already contain the earlier source-assisted image prime.

The cut is step 2/joint 1 entry. All non-image/text payload slots must match between P and Q, as must model identity and the complete execution point. Starting from Q, two typed Acts install complete image and text streams:

| Branch | Image source | Text source | Evidence partition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I.P_T.P | P | P | Known P replay |
| I.P_T.Q | P | Q | New hybrid |
| I.Q_T.P | Q | P | New hybrid |
| I.Q_T.Q | Q | Q | Known Q replay |

Every branch executes its own unchanged native suffix for 54 navigation operations and 49 transformer blocks, resets base conditioning before step 3 projection, and ends through the original scheduler and VAE. There is no new prompt encoding, noise draw or priming prefix.

Before any new target block, one MDB transaction freezes all supplied entry states and the prediction that I.Q_T.P has greater signed RGB displacement along the natural source-minus-base axis than I.P_T.Q in each context. The separate semantic prediction is that QP follows P's assignment and PQ follows Q's assignment, retaining the partial paraphrase. Exact new-hybrid pixels are not predicted.

## Observed assignments

All eight new hybrids follow the text-side assignment. In anchor and seed 347, QP gives a blue sphere and red cube while PQ gives a red sphere and blue cube. In the cone/cylinder scene QP gives a blue/violet truncated cone and red cylinder while PQ retains a red truncated cone and blue cylinder. The paraphrase's QP sphere remains purple and its cube red; PQ retains a red sphere and blue cube.

Same-text hybrids are very close to their diagonal references: RGB RMS differences are .352–.603 on the 0–255 scale, versus 42.861–50.607 for the conditional text effects. Subtle pixel/appearance differences remain, so categorical assignment agreement is not exact image equality. Cone truncation is an existing geometry limitation, not evidence of a substantial hybrid-induced shape change. Root and Astra independently inspected all sixteen native image cells; this is unblinded qualitative evidence rather than calibrated semantic certification.

Within the tested pair, T.P suffices for the P-like assignment under I.Q, and replacing it with T.Q removes that assignment under I.P. The result weakens image-state dominance or a requirement for exact original I/T pairing at this clock. It does not show that T.P suffices under an unprimed or arbitrary image state, or that image state has no computational role.

## Carrier and continuation mathematics

Write the cut state as S=(I,T,R), where R contains the common latent, conditioning, clock, positions and other declared state. For the native suffix F,

```text
Y_ab = F(I_a, T_b, R),   a,b in {P,Q}.
```

The intervention supplies both I_a and T_b. It does not predict how joint0 computed them. The full entry witnesses verify those supplied bits and typed writes. There are zero prospective learned-denoiser-equation or pixel numerical forecasts in this assay.

The finite text effects are Y_PP−Y_PQ at fixed I.P and Y_QP−Y_QQ at fixed I.Q. Image effects are Y_PP−Y_QP at fixed T.P and Y_PQ−Y_QQ at fixed T.Q. Their RGB interaction is

```text
J = Y_PP - Y_PQ - Y_QP + Y_QQ.
```

These are complete image-array contrasts. Their signed projection on a fixed natural scene difference is a diagnostic, not a semantic mediation percentage. The scalar used for the frozen ordering is

```text
s(Y) = <Y - Y_QQ, Y_natural-source - Y_natural-base>
       / ||Y_natural-source - Y_natural-base||^2.
```

All four frozen ordinal predictions hold:

| Context | I.Q_T.P | I.P_T.Q | Signed difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anchor | .652721 | .000886 | .651834 |
| Seed 347 | .617898 | .000387 | .617511 |
| Paraphrase | .525654 | .000370 | .525284 |
| Cone/cylinder | .492164 | .000281 | .491883 |

These numbers describe the fixed natural RGB direction relative to the known Q endpoint, not the probability or fraction of successful binding. The full audit also retains image/text conditional contrasts, interactions, per-reference RGB distances and tensor trajectories.

The saved trajectory also localizes the persistence boundary. Before project3 and immediately after its base-C reset, all branches have absent working image/text slots and differ only in latent among the complete declared payload. Projection rebuilds identical working text in all four branches. The next joint block makes text diverge again while reading the differing image/latent histories.

Schematically, within the native state closure:

```text
(I2,T2) -> native remaining step2 blocks -> noise prediction -> Z3
(Z3,C_base) -> (I3,identical T3) -> joint0 -> (I3',different T3').
```

This supports a temporary-text-to-latent handoff. Z3 is the sole varying persistent payload slot at that boundary; its differences include geometry, shading and branches with the same assignment. This does not identify a minimal semantic latent code or imply every latent difference implements binding. Later text divergence is newly computed, not survival of the old T tensor.

For the time-circuit account, a computation record needs its entry conditions, current carrier, clock, reader/writer and continuation. The symbol table should describe a conditional transition and its surviving consequences. A token row or vector alone omits the receiver and the later carrier change.

## Next discriminating cut

The full-T transplant contains both the changed input residual and new joint0 writes. It therefore cannot yet say where the useful text-side state was formed. Define S as the union of the original A and B row supports: four rows per context. Hold I.Q fixed and cross T.P versus T.Q on S and its complement. Use whole T.P and whole T.Q as known controls, adding only two new support/complement hybrids per case.

First verify that the earlier joint0 input states were identical outside S. Complement-only transfer would then identify causal carriage in differences newly produced outside the initially changed rows. Support-only transfer would leave carried residual versus rewritten state on S unresolved. Neither hybrid transferring would suggest coupling; both transferring would suggest conditional redundancy. The row set is determined from the sealed input intervention, before future outcomes.
