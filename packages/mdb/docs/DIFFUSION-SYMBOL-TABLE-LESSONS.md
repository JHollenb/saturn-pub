# What the diffusion experiments teach us about computation records

The [six-panel campaign](FLUX-COMPUTATION-FAMILIES.md) supports a bounded, relation-sensitive response-family candidate at diffusion step 0, single block 4 of FLUX.2 Klein 4B. Source residual and attention replacement together restore ordering across five directed relation contrasts; either replacement alone preserves recipient ordering. Color and shape source assignments are not restored. These observations make applicability and counterexamples part of the useful computation record.

The campaign executed 47 dependent native futures in one model process, with 48 rewinds and 39 typed Acts. Nine histories and seven directed pairs span development seed 37, held-out novel-object seed 53 and held-out third-object seed 59. The [portable index](../qualification/flux-six-panel-family-20261102.json) binds all rows, native images, seals, audit and manual visual observations. The records are curated family candidates; they have not been automatically imported into the equation atlas or promoted to semantic instructions.

## Findings that change the research workflow

| Observation | Consequence for a computation record |
|---|---|
| RA restores ordering in hybrid, ordinary, reciprocal, novel-object and third-object contrasts. | Record a recurring response with explicit tested histories and dependent denominators. |
| RA retains recipient color and shape assignments despite favorable numerical ranks. | Preserve numerical movement and semantic outcome separately, including counterexamples. |
| K and V both restore ordering under source residual; K beats V numerically. | Keep the source split and receiving-state conditions; retain the failed V-dominance guess. |
| Source queries preserve recipient ordering with recipient residual retained. | Do not transfer a sufficiency result from the restored-residual condition without testing it. |
| Top text-energy support loses to its alternative. | Use activation contrast to propose interventions, then measure causal effects through the native consumer. |
| Early selected-row repair is closer to the clean latent than late repair; both restore ordering. | Bind effects and collateral to the clock, rather than reducing repair to one semantic boolean. |
| Hybrid and natural working ports agree while stored conditioning differs and later futures diverge. | Include persistent sources that future transitions reread. |
| Prefix selectors choose RA and tie fixed RA where they act. | Require a differentiated-action comparison before claiming a useful route selector. |

The five relation pairs share histories and one checkpoint. New objects and seeds vary together across held-out pairs. Ordering is clearer than exact primitive geometry in assistant inspection. Absolute position, relative position, object binding and broader coherent spatial restoration remain competing interpretations.

## Structural law and empirical family

The structural writer is the native merge:

```text
R_next = R + gate * Linear(concat(A, M), W_merge, bias)
```

The intervention replaces source image-row R and/or A while retaining recipient MLP operands, gate, post-block text and stored conditioning. The worker retains the full projection shape for BF16 rounding. At fixed gate and MLP, the local real-arithmetic R/A changes are affine. The later nonlinear suffix can nevertheless produce an interaction between the separately tested interventions. That downstream response is the empirical family; the architecture equation alone does not establish a relation operator.

The numerical axis is source=0 and recipient=1 along their final latent difference. It is a signed projection, not semantic accuracy or the fraction of an image restored. All seven pairs satisfy the frozen RA-below-R-and-A rank. Color.RA and shape.RA remain semantic counterexamples. Native image inspection and target/collateral measurements stay separate from this scalar.

The earlier [four-future forecast](FLUX-BRANCH-FORECASTS.md) establishes exact conditional image calculations under its declared profile. This campaign establishes causal response and empirical applicability measurements. Its selectors do not independently calculate every edited RGB value.

## Record requirements for further discovery

Use these fields when curating or comparing the next observations. This is research guidance for campaign metadata, not a new runtime schema or a claim that the equation atlas already imports arbitrary causal records.

- **Identity and occurrence:** checkpoint, execution profile, seed/context, diffusion step, block and source-qualified generic IDs.
- **State and applicability:** observed prefix, persistent conditioning, receiving residual, retained operands and declared applicability conditions.
- **Address and action:** source cuts, image/text rows or heads, ports, sign/dose, typed Act and unchanged-state closure.
- **Carrier and consumer:** writer, carrier trajectory, later rereads, repair boundaries and own-state native continuation.
- **Effects and exceptions:** signed numerical effects, inspected ordering or assignments, geometry/palette collateral, controls and prediction misses.
- **Evidence status:** immutable provenance, prediction seal, audit scope, observation/family-candidate status and unresolved alternatives.

Existing [generic observations](../../../../artifacts/mdb-flux-six-panel-family-20261102/final-v1/computation-records.json) retain 18 source-qualified records. The [equation atlas](COMPUTATION-ATLAS.md) supplies exact-context retrieval and caller-selected comparisons for its supported equation catalog records. Automatic causal-record ingestion, cross-run grouping, transferable rule induction and semantic naming remain integration work. An unknown applicability field should remain explicit rather than silently inherit a nearby observation's scope.

## The next discriminating experiments

1. Match the receiving residual while comparing Q, K and V across additional ordinary histories. Test candidate text supports against alternative and matched controls through the native image suffix.
2. Hold seed and object inventory fixed while separating relative ordering, joint absolute translation and identity/binding changes. Track ordering, assignment, shape, scale and palette independently.
3. Select new development and held-out states where different actions win. Freeze route and signed-effect predictions before execution; compare with fixed RA, preserve abstention and run every declared arm.
4. Connect curated causal records to atlas retrieval with exact model/source/state bindings and separate structural-law, empirical-family and semantic interpretations. Validate the importer independently of any scientific rule promotion.
