usage: publish_qwen_sign_shortcut.py [-h] --pack PACK --out OUT
                                     --collection-receipt COLLECTION_RECEIPT
                                     --qualification QUALIFICATION
                                     --expected-manifest EXPECTED_MANIFEST
                                     --expected-request EXPECTED_REQUEST
                                     --expected-job EXPECTED_JOB

Publish externally authenticated compact Qwen sign-shortcut observations. Only
JSON/scalar evidence and source/checksum files are read. No saved tensor,
model, tokenizer, parameter, neural recurrence or original primitive is
loaded. An expected compact manifest/job/request must come from authenticated
collection.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --pack PACK
  --out OUT
  --collection-receipt COLLECTION_RECEIPT
  --qualification QUALIFICATION
  --expected-manifest EXPECTED_MANIFEST
  --expected-request EXPECTED_REQUEST
  --expected-job EXPECTED_JOB
