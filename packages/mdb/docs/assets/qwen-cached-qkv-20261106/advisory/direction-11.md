# Direction after holistic review 11

Turn14 strengthens the computation account by giving it a sharp counterexample. The complete functional block still predicts the native edited computation exactly. What fails is the cheaper reuse relation: a positive suffix row cannot reuse its positive attention-derived R/G/U merely because its incoming H18 row was unchanged. Its causal reads include the edited prefix, and those reads change the state that the MLP consumes.

I independently hashed all 40 compact data members and verified the 4,246-byte wrapper, giving 41 files and 1,676,498 bytes. Recounting individual saved fields gives eight primary full-functional/native comparisons over 12,058,624 positions, with exact endpoints and complete signed responses. The four positive baseline fields also match. The shortcut has 3,002,798 numerical endpoint discrepancies under the prefix edit, all in the later 256 rows. Each later-row head/R/M/Hnext shortcut response is zero while the actual response is nonzero, so each response-relative error is exactly one. These are dependent coordinate accounts, not millions of independent trials.

The latest post needs no material numerical or interpretive correction. It correctly distinguishes the roughly 1.27% whole-residual endpoint errors from missing the entire suffix response, and it retains 903 global and 415 prefix residual signed-zero defects. It also correctly says that the positioned-QKV finite premise remains to be tested. Naming the source's per-head Q/K RMS normalizations explicitly would make the derivation more executable; their ideal oddness preserves its conclusion.

The support argument must keep its precise scope. When every permitted key shares the query sign, ideal logits are unchanged and the weighted value sum acquires that common sign. Mixed support removes that sufficient guarantee. It does not force a visible effect at every row or head: cancellation, rounding and effectively silent reads remain possible. The prefix and alternating64 edits both have 65,536 cross-sign permitted edges but only 256 and 64 closed rows respectively. Edge count is therefore not an adequate description of the live computation, and no structural edge count measures attention mass or causal importance.

Cut15 is the right next experiment. It tests the missing source transformation rather than repeating the failed shortcut. The inspected candidate takes inherited own-functional positioned Q/K/V, applies the fixed row signs, and recomputes attention and every downstream write. It does not retain stale positive R/G/U. The explicit restoration of original noncontiguous BHTD strides is scientifically relevant: earlier work already showed that finite attention representation and dispatch can change a response even when the symbolic operation appears equivalent. Keep transformed, directly formed and actually consumed native QKV separate, then retain the three output and signed-response channels. The selected last-row and alternating64 masks are sufficient for this question; neither needs outcome-based replacement.

This implementation is still a proposed original-data test. Its CPU fixture can establish serialization, layout restoration, guards and accounting mechanics. It cannot establish CUDA parity on the original operands. The current source also represents a bounded all-attended causal enable-GQA scaffold, not a general cache/mask/profile resolver. Preserve that distinction when reporting the implementation.

The cumulative symbol-table account is now concrete. A record identifies an operation, its parameter instance, ports, numerical profile, operand provenance and applicability evidence. The live residuals, conditioning, latents, masks, positions, clock and any cache supply the state. A continuation identifies the next consumer. The record does not replace that state, and a generic name is sufficient. The twelve successful retrieval/abstention checks establish conservative lookup of occurrences, including the counterexample; they do not infer applicability for a new occurrence.

The broader operational account is substantial. Earlier work forms its own states from raw inputs, follows the denoising schedule, predicts decoder outputs and tests actual consumed interventions. The E18/H27 experiments distinguish a direct conditioning constituent from inherited history. The sign experiments add an explanatory local relation and now a measured limit of its reuse. These are different achievements from independently implementing CUDA arithmetic or assigning human semantic names. Neither of those stronger achievements is an automatic completion requirement.

Retain the adverse history alongside that progress. Independent arithmetic previously matched large endpoints while missing small responses badly. Intended semantic transfers did not consistently occur. FLUX writer rankings reversed after the next consumer, and absolute response growth sometimes accompanied declining relative response. Decoder export/recovery limitations and signed-zero defects remain genuine limitations of their original records. Later exact shared-primitive forecasts do not retrospectively repair them.

After cut15, consolidate the relations into one evaluator that separates structural eligibility, measured finite coverage and source/profile support. Exercise that evaluator first against the existing saved occurrences and unsupported metadata cases. Its decision must follow declared inputs and prerequisites, not observed target error. A supported path may compute the cheap relation or recompute attention; an unsupported path should carry an explicit abstention or qualified fallback. Numerical misses should remain observations with all rows retained, not become new discovery gates.

Then use a native consumer only where it can distinguish alternatives. Turn14 already supplies two different H19 states: the cheap prediction and the correctly recomputed prefix-edited state. One block19 continuation can test whether their discrepancy persists, spreads or is reduced, while prospectively checking the complete H20 difference. This is a meaningful next cut because the local miss is real and the cumulative research shows that consumption can change an effect's importance. Another full image from identical corrected states would add no discrimination.

The active objective should remain bounded. The tested raw-to-image program can already be described as operationally accounted for within its declared source and primitive domain. The remaining work concerns reusable applicability and the consequences of counterfactual differences. What would materially change this account is a finite QKV-transformation miss, a mismatch despite matched operands/profile, an evaluator that silently selects unsupported reuse, or a downstream consumer that changes the local-error interpretation. None requires an unlimited benchmark, compressed state or a semantic dictionary. This review anchors completed turn14; review again by turn17, or immediately after a major counterexample.
