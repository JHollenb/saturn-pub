## Fixed setup

FLUX.2 Klein 4B distilled, BF16, TF32 off, 256 px, 4 steps, B1, seed 4242 only (single seed by default). Prompts and words as the 2026-11-11 read-graph experiment and the read-panel lane: `cat` = "a photorealistic black cat sitting beside a red ball in fresh snow at dawn, soft blue light" (red, black, ball, snow), `car` = "a small blue car parked under a large oak tree in heavy rain at night" (night, rain). Every word is one text row. Cuts go through `mdb_lanes.attention_port` (precision `native`, zero method `delta`, renormalising cuts recomputed in FP32), arms through the unchanged `qualification/flux_read_panel_worker.py`: cut arms run under the `iso:<w>` conditioning and cut the reads of `own:<w>` by the receivers `image` and `others:<w>` (all text rows except the word), exactly as the lane's own60 arms.

## Identity under test

One head, receiver t, source set S, native FP32 softmax weights p, per-head values v, head output a = p·v, p_S = sum over S of p_j. Zero cut: a' − a = −sum_S p_j v_j. Mask cut: a' − a = (p_S a − sum_S p_j v_j)/(1 − p_S). Residual write change = g ⊙ O_h (a' − a), with g the block's outer attention gate (joint blocks: image gate with `to_out[0]`, text gate with `to_add_out`; single blocks: one gate, attention columns of the fused `to_out`).

## Experiment A: predicted write versus mass

**A0, instrument (smoke, before anything else).** Word snow (smoke) then red and snow (capture job). Cells (step, block) = (0,1), (0,4), (1,7), (2,14), (3,22); head = the cell's largest PW_zero head for that word. Each cell is cut alone (one head, one step, one block) for sources = the word's row and = all 512 text rows, in both cut types, plus the renormalising sham. Measured: the change of the attention output map's input (head outputs) and output (the write before the gate) against the identity computed from the same call's native q, k, v. Pass criterion, stated before the data: (i) zero cut: every element of the head-output change within half a BF16 ulp of the new value (the delta cut rounds once); (ii) mask cut: every element within |a_FP32 − a_native| + half an ulp (the recompute replaces the native kernel's rounding); (iii) every other head unchanged (max |change| = 0); (iv) the gated write change through the BF16 output map within relative error 0.05 of the predicted write wherever the predicted write exceeds 10× the sham's write change; cells below that are reported as floor-limited. If (i)–(iii) fail, stop and report.

**Scores (one native capture, no cut runs).** From the capture job's iso-conditioning run: per (block, head), PW_zero and PW_mask = sum over the 4 steps and over the receivers `image` and `others:<w>` of the norm of the gated predicted write change for cutting the word's row (zero and mask identity). PW_both = PW_zero/sum(PW_zero) + PW_mask/sum(PW_mask). Mass = the lane's existing definition, unchanged: each word's write-mass share per unit, `flux_read_panel_stage.word_masses()` (full-a capture, native conditioning, seed 4242: mean over image receivers of p_tj·|O_h v_j|, summed over steps and the word's row, normalised). own60 = the existing set (`head_sets3.json`, top 60 of share·log(share/rest), a contrast on the same mass). Reported: Spearman over 600 units of PW_both, PW_zero, PW_mask against mass; top-60 overlaps pw60/own60, pw60/mass-top60. If overlap(pw60, own60) > 50 of 60, I say plainly that the two rankings are not separable by top-60 and only the dissociation arms matter.

**Sets (rules fixed now; code `plans.py`, selection seed 20261113).**
- pw60: top 60 by PW_both.
- own60: existing.
- massmatch-lowpw: 60 units with total mass within 10% (relative) of own60's total mass, minimising total PW_both (`predicted_write.matched_min`: Lagrangian start, then single swaps to a 1-swap local optimum).
- pwmatch-lowmass: 60 units with total PW_both within 10% of pw60's, minimising total mass (same search).
- rand60a, rand60b: `attention_ranking.matched_control_sets`, target pw60, joint/single counts and distinct blocks per stratum matched, disjoint, excluding every named set of the word (pw60, own60, massmatch-lowpw, pwmatch-lowmass) and, for rand60b, rand60a. Seeds `draw_seed(20261113, word, name, "cut")`.
- keep-pw60, keep-own60, keep-rand60a: cut every unit except the set.
- F3 sets: per word, 16 sets of 10 units drawn uniformly without replacement from the union of the top 100 by PW_both and the top 100 by mass (seed `draw_seed(20261113, word, "f3", "cut")`).

**Arms.** Plan `main`: the 9 sets above per word in both cut types, every arm with the lane's floors (`sham_recompute` and `cut_recompute` for zero cuts, `sham` for mask cuts), the all-cut references `z:none` and `m:none` with floors, `iso` and `del`; 312 branches, two leases. Plan `f3`: 16 sets per word in both cut types, no per-arm floors (waived; the main plan's floors give the scale), with iso, del, z:none, m:none; 216 branches, one lease.

**Effect metric.** gone_t = (d(arm) − d(iso)) / (d(t:none) − d(iso)), d = 1 − MAD(arm, del)/MAD(native, del), pixel MAD on the decoded 256 px image, t:none = the all-cut of the arm's own cut type. Secondary: px MAD from iso. Floor bound in gone units = floor px / (MAD(native, del)·|span|).

**Forecasts (decision rules).**
- F1: massmatch-lowpw removes less than one third of what own60 removes, gone(massmatch-lowpw) < gone(own60)/3, for red, black and ball, each cut type. Held iff all 6 cells hold.
- F2: pwmatch-lowmass removes at least two thirds of what pw60 removes, gone(pwmatch-lowmass) ≥ 2/3·gone(pw60), all 6 words × 2 types. Held iff all 12 cells hold.
- F3: on the F3 sets, the per-set predicted write of the arm's cut type (sum of unit PW_zero for zero cuts, PW_mask for mask cuts) orders gone with a higher Spearman than mass does. Per (word, type) cell, 16 sets. Held iff the mean over the 12 cells of (Spearman_PW − Spearman_mass) > 0 and PW is higher in at least 8 of 12 cells.
- F4: each random draw removes less than one third of pw60: gone(rand60x) < gone(pw60)/3, 6 words × 2 types × 2 draws. Held iff all 24 cells hold.

**What refutes "predicted write is the missing variable".** F1 failing (equal mass with low predicted write still removes the word: mass carries effect PW does not see), or F2 failing (equal PW with low mass loses most of the effect: PW is not sufficient). Verdict for the hypothesis, from the fixed list: `supported` iff F1, F2, F3 and F4 all hold; `not_supported` iff F1 and F2 both fail; `set_only` iff F4 holds (pw60 is needed beyond a random set of its size and strata) but F1 or F2 fails; `unresolved` iff the dissociation sets overlap their reference so much that the comparison is empty (overlap(pwmatch-lowmass, pw60) > 50 or overlap(massmatch-lowpw, own60) > 50) or the relevant gone differences lie inside their floor bounds; `control_missing` iff a random draw could not be made.

**Qwen side check.** The value-coalition case of `docs/QWEN-PANELS.md` (FP32). For each of the 18 value cells (layer 19–27 × KV group), the exact first-hop write change at the first receiver (row 21), PW = sum over the group's 6 query heads of p[l,h,21,18]·W_o,h (v_donor − v_recipient), float64 (the head-mix formula of `mdb.analysis.reads`), its norm, and its read-out onto hot − cold through the final norm at the native final state. Reported against the saved FP32 singleton effects and complement losses (`qualification/qwen_panels_20261112/full-tables.json`), Pearson, Spearman and leave-one-out, next to max source attention (saved Spearman 0.360 / 0.263). Forecast Q1: Spearman(PW norm, |singleton|) and Spearman(PW read-out, singleton) both exceed Spearman(max source attention, singleton) = 0.36. One job.

## Experiment B: routing or payload

Per word, two captures at matched geometry: the native prompt (word present) and the word-deleted prompt (`flux_read_panels.delete_word`, the earlier experiment's construction). Deletion shifts rows: the alignment is the earlier experiment's hybrid alignment (rows before the word unchanged; later real rows and padding shift by the deleted token count); the word's row is paired with the one trailing padding row the shift frees (`predicted_write.deletion_alignment`). Image rows align one to one. For every (step, block), image receivers, keys aligned: P_d V_d − P_n V_n = dP·V_n + P_n·dV + dP·dV, each term carried through the head output maps and the gate, summed over heads (the block's attention write). Reported per word (all blocks; joint and single separately; per step): signed projection shares onto the total change (routing / payload / interaction, summing to one) and norm ratios; within payload, the projection shares of the word row, other real text rows, padding rows and image rows; and the share of the text payload that is common to all text sources ((sum over text of P)·mean over the 512 text rows of dV) versus source-specific. Closure remainder of the FP32 split and against the native BF16 outputs; the invariant dP·V = dP·(V − 1 v̄ᵀ) checked numerically.

Forecasts, as orderings between group means (G1 = red, ball, black; G2 = snow, rain, night), on all blocks:
- G1: the payload change of G1 words is concentrated on few rows: mean(word + other-real payload share) of G1 > that of G2, and the common share of G1 < that of G2 (a larger source-specific part). Held iff both orderings hold.
- G2: snow, rain, night receive a payload change spread over many rows including padding: mean padding payload share of G2 > that of G1, and common share G2 > G1. Held iff both orderings hold.

**G3, one causal arm (value swap).** In the 20 single blocks, image receivers read the text-row values of the deleted-word run (aligned rows) instead of their own, through the live attention map of the run (routing kept; delta form: native output + P_text·(V_del − V)); text receivers and joint blocks untouched; native conditioning. Sub-arms: all text rows, the word's row only, every text row but the word's; a recompute sham (FP32 P·V, nothing replaced) as the floor; and the all-rows swap under the iso conditioning (gone on the zero-cut scale of the main plan). Words snow and rain (forecast), red and ball (contrast). d = 1 − MAD(arm, del)/MAD(native, del) (0 = native, 1 = deleted). Forecast G3: for snow and rain, the all-rows swap gives d ≥ 0.7 and the picture shows the ground snow / the rain gone, where head cuts of ≤ 240 heads did not (own240 gone 0.45 snow / 0.22 rain at seed 4242, zero cut, the lane's table). Held iff both words meet d ≥ 0.7 and the picture judgement agrees. G3b (sub-arm reading): for snow and rain the every-row-but-the-word swap moves the image further toward the deletion (larger d) than the word-row-only swap.
